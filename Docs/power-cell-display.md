# Power Cell display

The Blender casing is 0.45 m wide, 0.45 m deep and 1 m tall. Its origin is halfway between the two ends. The editable core measures 0.314 × 0.314 × 0.576 m, and both objects have unit scale and one UV map. Reimporting the FBX in Blender produced matching dimensions for each object. Four casing materials and two core materials map onto the original Unity palette.

The preview in RepairerDemo uses unit scale and puts the model centre 0.5 m above the floor. Lower emission on the core ribs preserves darker vertical stripes against the brighter cyan core. Press L to compare the normal directional light with reduced lighting. The display model is separate from the three interactive factory pickups.

In FacilityEscape_Main, start the mission and collect the cells in areas A, B and C. The counter advances once for each object. The third cell unlocks the terminal; face it and press E to extract.
